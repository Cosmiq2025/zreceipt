How it works

  1. Pay as usual

    Send a shielded payment from any Zcash wallet. Put the invoice number or purpose in the memo.

  2. Create a receipt

    Paste the transaction ID and your read-only viewing key. The receipt is made in your browser, and the key never leaves it.

  3. Send the link

    The recipient's browser fetches the transaction from the blockchain and checks the receipt itself. A changed amount or a forged receipt fails.

What the receipt shows, and what it doesn't

Disclosed

The amount, the recipient's address, the memo, and the transaction and block it was mined in.

Withheld

Your balance, every other payment you have made or received, your own address, and the other outputs in the same transaction.

Why it holds

The receipt is a one-time key for a single output. The verifier recomputes that output's commitment from the chain, so the numbers cannot be edited.

Who it is for

Businesses

Pay staff and suppliers without publishing your payroll, and still answer an audit request in a minute.

Auditors and accountants

Get proof of a specific payment that you can verify yourself, without asking for access to the whole wallet.

Exchanges and banks

Accept shielded deposits with a source-of-funds proof that covers the payment in question and nothing more.
