Three steps, no account

  1. Pay as usual

    Send a shielded payment from your Zcash wallet. Put the invoice number or purpose in the memo.

  2. Create a receipt

    Paste the transaction ID and your viewing key. The receipt is made in your browser, and the key never leaves it.

  3. Send the link

    The recipient's browser fetches the transaction from the blockchain and checks the receipt itself. An edited amount or a forged receipt fails.

Businesses
Pay staff and suppliers without publishing your payroll, and still answer an audit request in a minute.

Banks and exchanges

Why the result can be trusted

Checked by the verifier

The recipient's browser recomputes the payment from the public transaction. Nobody has to trust this website.

Cannot be edited

A receipt opens exactly one output. Change the amount, address or memo and the check against the chain fails.

Your key stays with you

The viewing key is used only in your browser to make the receipt. It is never sent, and a receipt cannot spend funds.

Open and standard

Open source. Implements payment disclosure from the Zcash proposal ZIP 311, for the Sapling, Orchard and Ironwood pools.
