How it works

No account and no upload. The receipt is made in your browser and checked in theirs.

  1. 1

    Pay privately

    From any shielded Zcash wallet. Put the invoice number in the memo.

  2. 2

    Create the receipt

    Paste the transaction ID and your viewing key. The key never leaves your browser.

  3. 3

    Share one link

    They open it and their browser checks the blockchain.

Try it on a real payment

This receipt is for a payment we made on Zcash mainnet, in the Ironwood pool. Open it and your browser rebuilds the payment from the public transaction. Change one character and it fails.

What they see, and what they don't

Shown

  • Amount
  • Recipient
  • Memo
  • Block and date

Kept private

  • Your balance
  • Other payments
  • Your address
  • Rest of the transaction

Who it's for

Businesses

Keep payroll and suppliers private, and answer an audit request with one link.

Auditors and accountants

Check the payment you asked about against the blockchain. No screenshots, no wallet access.

Banks and exchanges

Confirm the source of funds for one deposit without seeing the client's whole wallet.

Privacy by default. Disclosure by choice.
